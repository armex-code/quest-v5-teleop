Checking WebXR support…

In AR: hold the right controller at the near-left corner of your drawing area and press A, then at the far-right corner and press B. After that the arm follows the controller; trigger = pen down. Left grip grabs the digital twin, right stick click clears the trail.

No controllers? Bare hands work: right index fingertip is the pen (pinch = pen down). Left-hand pinches: index = corner A, middle = corner B, ring (hold) = move the twin, pinky = clear the trail.
